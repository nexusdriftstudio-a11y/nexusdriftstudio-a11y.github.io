Privacy Policy for StepLock Pro

Last Updated: April 08, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StepLock Pro ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. StepLock Pro operates primarily offline and does not require you to create an account.

1. Data We Access and How We Use It

A. Health Connect Data (Step Count & Distance)

StepLock Pro requests access to your health data (specifically, Steps and Distance) via Google's Health Connect API.

  • How we use it: We read your step count solely to power the core functionality of the app—unlocking your restricted apps once you reach your daily walking goals.
  • Storage & Protection: The Health Connect data we access is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your health data to our servers, nor do we store it in any cloud database.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or transfer your Health Connect data to data brokers, advertising networks, or any other third parties. The data is strictly used on your device to calculate your progress.

B. AccessibilityService API (App Locking Functionality)

StepLock Pro requires the use of Android's AccessibilityService API.

  • How we use it: We use this service strictly to detect which app is currently in the foreground on your device. This allows us to enforce the "App Lock" feature by displaying a blocker screen if you try to open an app you restricted before reaching your step goal.
  • Storage & Protection: We do not record your screen, do not log your keystrokes, and do not collect the content of the apps you use. The foreground app detection happens entirely locally in real-time and the data is immediately discarded.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

While we do not collect personal data on our servers, StepLock Pro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

  • AdMob may automatically collect certain device information, such as your IP address, device type, network information, and your Android Advertising ID, to serve relevant ads.
  • AdMob operates according to its own privacy policy. You can learn more about how Google handles data in its advertising products here: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because you do not create an account and all your personal and health data (steps, restricted apps list) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data.

  • To delete your data: Simply clear the app's cache/data in your Android settings, or uninstall StepLock Pro from your device. This will permanently delete all your app-related data.

4. Children's Privacy

StepLock Pro is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
