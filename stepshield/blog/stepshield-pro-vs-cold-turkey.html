StepShield Pro vs Cold Turkey: Mobile vs Desktop Blocker

Published July 16, 2026 · NexusDrift Studio

These two tools solve different problems. One lives in your pocket and makes you walk; the other lives on your computer and locks you out on a schedule. Here's the honest comparison.

The short version

  • StepShield Pro is an Android app: pedometer + app locker that blocks distracting apps until you hit your step goal (Walk to Unlock).
  • Cold Turkey is a Desktop blocker (Windows/macOS, plus browser extension): blocks websites & apps on a timer or schedule, famous for hardcore anti-bypass.

They're not really competitors — they're complementary. But if you have to choose where to start, the answer depends on where your distraction lives.

Side by side

Dimension StepShield Pro Cold Turkey
Platform Android only Windows, macOS, browser extension
Core mechanic Walk-to-Unlock: apps locked until step goal met Schedule/timer locks websites & apps
Best at stopping Phone scrolling (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) Desktop time-sinks (social, games, news)
Anti-bypass Strong on-device; accessibility permission revocable by determined user; offers uninstall protection via device-admin OS-level locks widely considered hardest to voluntarily bypass
Privacy model All data local on device; no cloud sync Local-first; some cloud features for sync
Cost 100% free forever — contains ads, no paid tier Paid (one-time desktop license)

Where StepShield Pro wins

  • Movement as the reward, not punishment. Most blockers just say "no." StepShield Pro makes screen time something you earn.
  • Actually private. No account, no login, no telemetry. Compare that to blockers that sync everything to the cloud.
  • No dark patterns. There is no "premium unlock" that silently disables your block list. Free means free.
  • Works offline. Blocking and step counting are local — no server round-trips, no downtime.

Where Cold Turkey wins

  • Desktop coverage. StepShield doesn't touch your laptop — Cold Turkey locks the whole machine.
  • Bypass resistance. Cold Turkey's OS-level locks are widely considered the hardest to voluntarily defeat.
  • Mature scheduling. Recurring weekly blocks, locked modes, frozen mode that survives restarts.

Common myths busted

"StepShield Pro blocks websites too." No — StepShield is an app locker plus pedometer. It gates apps on your phone, not web pages on your computer. Cold Turkey works on Android. Cold Turkey Blocker's core is desktop; they offer a browser extension, but there's no full Android app locker equivalent to StepShield.

What should you pick?

  • Distracted mainly by your phone → start with StepShield Pro.
  • Distracted mainly by your computer → start with Cold Turkey.
  • Both → run both; they don't overlap.
