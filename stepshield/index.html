What is an app blocker with a pedometer?

An Android app that gates distracting apps behind a daily walking goal. StepShield Pro counts your steps and unlocks blocked apps once you reach the target.

How does StepShield Pro unlock apps by walking?

Choose the apps to block and a daily step goal. When the goal is met, the apps unlock for the rest of the day. No goal, no unlock — no workarounds.

Yes — 100% free forever, contains ads, no paid tier, zero analytics, no Firebase, no cloud sync. All step tracking and blocking stay on your device.

Any installed app: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Twitter/X, browsers, games, messaging. You build the block list yourself.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Step counting and blocking are fully on-device and work offline — no server round-trips, no downtime.
